Who We Are

MIDMEN Execution Network ("MIDMEN", "we", "us" or "our") operates a service-request and coordination platform focused initially on Zimbabwe. We help customers request, arrange and monitor approved tasks through MIDMEN personnel and, where relevant, third-party execution partners. For privacy enquiries, contact hello@midmen.co. The full registered legal entity name and any legally required business identification will be displayed once verified against the company registration records.

What We Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • (a) your name, email address, telephone number and preferred contact method;
  • (b) task descriptions, service locations, recipient details and instructions you choose to submit;
  • (c) photographs, documents, receipts and other materials you provide or that are reasonably necessary to evidence a task;
  • (d) quotation, transaction and payment-status records (payment card or banking credentials should be processed by authorized payment providers rather than collected through ordinary request forms);
  • (e) communications, complaints and feedback; and
  • (f) technical data such as IP address, device/browser information and necessary website logs.

Please avoid sending sensitive medical, identity or financial information unless we have specifically requested it through an appropriate secure channel.

Why We Use It

We use information to review and respond to enquiries; assess feasibility; prepare quotations; coordinate approved services; communicate progress; verify delivery; maintain records; prevent fraud and misuse; protect users and our systems; comply with legal obligations; and improve our service.

We process information where necessary to respond to a request, perform an agreed service, meet legal duties, or pursue legitimate business interests, and seek consent where required. We do not sell customer personal information.

Sharing With Execution Partners

We share only information reasonably needed to perform an approved task with relevant MIDMEN personnel, vetted suppliers, delivery partners, qualified professionals and technology/payment providers. For example, a delivery partner may need the recipient's name, contact number and delivery location. We expect recipients to protect the information, use it only for the authorized purpose and comply with applicable law.

We may disclose information to competent authorities where legally required, or to protect safety and prevent fraud. Where data is processed outside Zimbabwe, we will apply legally required safeguards and assess applicable cross-border data protection requirements.

Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, service records, dispute handling and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type; they will be documented internally and applied consistently.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and secure transmission where available. No online system is completely risk-free. If we learn of a security incident, we will assess and respond in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information. Contact hello@midmen.co to exercise these rights.
