1. Cookie Notice

This notice explains how MIDMEN Execution Network uses cookies and similar technologies on midmen.co. Some are needed for core functionality; others, such as analytics or advertising tools, are optional and may require consent.

2. Current State

MIDMEN uses or may use essential technical functions required to deliver website pages, maintain security and operate enquiry forms. Before publishing this statement, the developer must inspect the actual website and confirm whether any cookies, local storage, embedded services or third-party tracking scripts are active. Do not claim "we use no cookies" or "we do not track visitors" without technical verification. Any active providers and their purposes should be listed accurately in the published notice.

If MIDMEN introduces Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising tags, embedded video or other non-essential tracking, we will update this notice to identify the tool, provider, purpose, categories of information and retention details as appropriate. Where required by applicable law, we will offer an accessible consent choice before enabling non-essential technologies and a way to revisit that choice. You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.
